Why it works

"The craving clock your body built around nicotine is a nonlinear attractor. You don't leap over it. You tilt the floor."

Homeostatic systems — cardiac, metabolic, neurochemical — follow cascade dynamics described by May (1976) and formalized by Feigenbaum (1978–79). Nicotine didn't destroy your endogenous nicotinamide production; it idled it. The sixty-second delay operates below the bifurcation threshold where the body mobilizes resistance, gradually recalibrating the craving interval until the attractor shifts. Not willpower. Physics.
